# SciProspect pricing: proposal (not live yet)

Status: **proposal pasted by the owner on 2026-11-23, under review.** Nothing in the product uses this yet; the live plans are still
Free, Pro $29, Business $59 and Organization $99 (see `backend/plans.py`). The source text wrote the name as "SisProspect"; it is
written here as SciProspect on the assumption that was a typo.

Find researchers and build your event website in one workspace. Choose Basic, Pro, Ultra, or Custom. All plans are available to
individuals, teams, and organizations. Prices are in USD per account.

## Start your 7-day free trial

Try SciProspect with one account for one user. Collect up to 1,000 new research contacts per day during the trial. To continue
after the 7 days, choose a paid plan. SciProspect has no permanent free plan.

## Included in every paid plan

- **Research collector**: discover publicly listed researcher contacts, review source evidence, check email domains, filter by
  delivery status, and export contacts to CSV.
- **Event website builder**: build pages with the visual editor, add speaker profiles, collect registrations, and accept abstract
  submissions.

Daily contact limits reset for each user. Actual collection results depend on your research topic and the availability of unique,
publicly listed email addresses.

Storage is shared across your workspace. File storage covers images, speaker photos, PDFs, and uploads. Data storage covers contact
records, source evidence, page content, and form responses. Customers who connect their own domain purchase and renew it separately.
