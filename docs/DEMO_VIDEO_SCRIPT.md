Record at 1080p. Caption = on-screen text. Voice = optional narration.

| # | Time | Show on screen | Caption / voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Title slide | "SciStudio: build your conference website without code." |

## Tips
- Use a clean browser window (hide bookmarks), zoom the page to 110–125% so text is readable.
- Move the mouse slowly; pause 1 second after each click.
- Add soft background music in Clipchamp; keep captions 2 lines max.
- Export: MP4, 1080p.
