# SciProspect & SciStudio: client demo deck (outline)

Turn this into slides next session (about 10 slides). Screenshots to add are marked [SCREENSHOT].

1. **Title:** SciStudio by SciProspect. Build your conference website without code.
2. **The problem:** Conference organisers juggle a website builder, registration forms, payment tools and spreadsheets. Generic builders (Wix, WordPress, Webflow) don't know conferences; event platforms are expensive.
3. **The solution:** One tool: a visual website builder made for scientific conferences, with registration, forms and SEO built in. No technical skills needed.
4. **Visual editor** [SCREENSHOT: editor]: Drag designs onto the page, click any text to edit, quick toolbar for fonts and colours, desktop/tablet/mobile views, undo/redo, light and dark mode.
5. **Ready-made sections:** Navigation, hero, speakers, agenda, pricing, FAQ, gallery, sponsors, contact forms (5 designs), popup forms, footer. All editable.
6. **Multi-page sites** [SCREENSHOT: pages]: Home, Program, Speakers, Registration, Venue. Linked navigation, preview of every page.
7. **Registration & payments** [SCREENSHOT: registration]: Registration types, early-bird prices that switch by date, add-ons (accommodation, guests), discount codes, secure server-side pricing, payment step (Stripe next).
8. **Forms & inbox** [SCREENSHOT: inbox]: Every submission saved securely, inbox with CSV export, custom fields per form.
9. **SEO & analytics:** Google search preview, page titles and descriptions, social share image, Google Analytics in one field.
10. **Secure & scalable:** Cloud PostgreSQL database, hashed sessions, validated inputs, version history of every site. Your own domain (coming).
11. **Plans** (example): Starter $19/mo · Pro $49/mo · Organization $149/mo · Enterprise custom. Per-event option $299. 14-day free trial.
12. **Roadmap:** Live publishing on custom domains, abstract submission and review, real payments, email notifications, team roles, SciProspect research data in the web app.
13. **Call to action:** Start a free trial / book a walkthrough. Contact details.
